Paper Dragon Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026
Android app: Paper Dragon
Package: com.paperdragon.geisharun

This policy explains how Paper Dragon and its integrated service providers handle information when you play the Android game or contact its developer. Developer and privacy contact details are provided at the end of this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores your unlocked levels, stars, winning health and score records, coins, booster inventory, tutorial progress, music and haptics preferences locally on your device. It also stores active play time and records needed to avoid granting the same advertising reward more than once. No game account or sign-in is required.

The gameplay itself does not ask you to enter your name, phone number, address or payment details, and does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise GPS location.

2. Information collected by integrated services

The Android app includes SayKit, including SayAnalytics, advertising and related service integrations. These services may collect and process:

  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system and app version, language, network information, IP address, approximate country or region inferred from the connection, app installation identifiers and advertising identifiers where available.
  • Gameplay and interaction information: sessions and play time, level starts and outcomes, scores, remaining health, tutorial steps, screen and button interactions, booster use, virtual coin earnings and spending, and advertising reward events.
  • Advertising and attribution information: ad requests, availability, impressions, clicks, completed rewarded ads, and information used to measure installations and advertising performance.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, errors, performance and technical logs needed to investigate failures and improve stability.

These categories can be associated with app, installation or device identifiers. They should not be treated as anonymous merely because the game has no account system. The exact information processed by an advertising partner depends on the SDK configuration, ads served, your device settings and applicable privacy choices.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to operate and improve the game, preserve progress and preferences, measure game balance and level difficulty, detect and fix technical problems, deliver and measure advertising, credit confirmed advertising rewards, prevent duplicate rewards or misuse, and respond to support or privacy requests. Advertising partners may use permitted identifiers and interactions to select ads, including personalized ads where allowed by your choices and applicable law.

4. Service providers and sharing

Information needed for those purposes is processed by the relevant SDK and infrastructure providers. The app uses or includes integrations for:

  • SayGames / SayKit / SayAnalytics: game analytics, SDK infrastructure, advertising integration and attribution. See the SayGames Privacy Policy, including its information about advertising and analytics partners.
  • Google services: Firebase analytics and crash reporting integrations, Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, and Google Play services, including the optional in-app review flow. See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.
  • Meta / Facebook SDK integration: app events and attribution. See the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising and mediation partners connected through SayKit: information about ad delivery and measurement is handled under the policies of the partners serving the ads, as described in the SayGames policy.

Information may also be disclosed when necessary to comply with applicable law, protect rights or security, or support a business transfer subject to appropriate protections. This game does not have public profiles, chat, or a feature for sharing your gameplay records with other players.

5. Your choices and local data

You can change music and haptics preferences in the game. Those controls affect sound and vibration; they do not disable analytics or advertising. Watching a rewarded ad is optional. Other advertising may be shown during play.

You can manage advertising privacy and identifier settings through your Android device settings. Available options and their names depend on your Android version. If an integrated provider presents additional consent or privacy controls, you can use those controls as offered. Removing or resetting an advertising identifier does not necessarily stop all technical, security or non-personalized advertising processing.

You can erase the game's local progress and preferences by clearing its app storage in Android settings or uninstalling it. This removes local progress and inventory and does not automatically delete information already processed by SDK providers.

6. Privacy requests and deletion

Contact the developer using the email below to ask about your information, request access, correction or deletion, or exercise other applicable privacy rights. State that your request concerns Paper Dragon and include the Android package name. If you know an app or advertising identifier relevant to your request, you may provide it so records can be located. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers or unnecessary personal information.

We will review requests in accordance with applicable law and may need information to locate the relevant records or verify the request. Some SDK-held information is managed by the provider under its own policy; we may refer you to that provider or coordinate the request where appropriate. You may also have the right to object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Retention, security and international processing

Local game data remains on the device until it is reset or removed. Information processed by service providers is retained for the purposes described above according to their policies, service settings and applicable legal requirements. The game does not set one universal retention period for all providers.

Services and their infrastructure may process information in countries other than your own. Applicable contractual and legal safeguards depend on the provider and destination. Network services use encrypted connections where supported and required; no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children

We do not ask players to submit their age or knowingly seek children's personal information in violation of applicable law. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information through the game or a connected service, contact us so the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action taken. Refer to the game's store listing for its age rating.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies this version. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
